## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished 12-panel storyboard for a long scroll-driven website animation. Reference image establishes the yellow tower crane design, pallet, warm ivory background and detailed realistic 3D illustration aesthetic. Expand to a clear 4-column by 3-row storyboard, landscape overall, high resolution. Each panel numbered 01 through 12 with small number only, no other text. Consistent same crane and same suspended pallet of pale construction blocks throughout. This is a premium rendered architectural illustration, not a photograph, not flat vector art. Clear physically plausible crane geometry and lifting cables.
Panels 01–07: ONLY isolated yellow tower crane and suspended pallet, NO construction site, NO buildings, NO scenery. 01 full crane hero three-quarter view with load clearly visible. 02 upper crane slews about its fixed vertical mast axis. 03 camera orbits to counterweight side, load remains visible. 04 dramatic low angle under boom with pallet foreground. 05 elevated view along lattice jib toward suspended load. 06 close three-quarter view of pallet and hook with crane behind. 07 wider new angle preparing to descend, still isolated crane and pallet. Make views varied yet coherent rather than repeated identical frames. Crane base stays anchored; it never flies randomly.
Panels 08–12: reveal a much larger and richer building construction site late in the narrative. Same coherent architectural complex of FOUR mid-rise apartment buildings under construction, approximately 6–10 floors, around an internal access lane. Different stages: exposed concrete frame and floor slabs; partial red brick infill with large facade openings; taller structure with scaffolding, timber formwork and projecting rebar. Construction details include edge protection, scaffold decks, a compact excavator, site containers, stacked pallets, a concrete mixer truck and fenced delivery entrance, all to scale. No detached houses, no suburban homes, no city skyline, no photographic surroundings. Isolated miniature architectural diorama on ivory. Crane appropriately taller than buildings.
08 first late reveal: foundations and incomplete lower structural frames begin appearing below the crane, wide elevated view. 09 developing complex with upper floors assembling, broader establishing view showing access lane and architectural depth. 10 full substantial site established; crane load above a clear receiving area on the upper open slab of nearest building. 11 closer view of pallet lowered just above that upper-floor slab, taut lifting slings, background buildings and scaffolding visible. 12 satisfying close final delivery: pallet resting flat on the same slab, lifting slings now slack, hook just above it, strong foreground columns and rails and layered mid-rise buildings behind.
Premium studio light, warm yellow painted steel, convincing concrete grain, red masonry, timber and steel details. Consistent scale, generous clean margins, thin subtle panel separators. Prioritize large readable illustrations over typography. No logos, no watermark.
