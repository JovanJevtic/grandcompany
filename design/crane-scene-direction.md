# Scenografija krana — koncept za pregled

Storyboard: crane-storyboard.png. Generisano ugrađenim Image Gen alatom na osnovu
img/crane-render.png i img/pallet-render.png. Ovo je koncept; nije ugrađen u sajt.

## Prostorna pravila

- Jedan prostor i jedno mjesto istovara tokom cijele scene.
- Kran je oslonjen na postolje; ne prelijeće stranicu.
- Paleta ima jednu jasnu vertikalnu putanju i unaprijed vidljivo odredište.
- U prvom viewportu vidi se cijela kompozicija, naslov i primarna akcija.
- Pozadina ostaje boja sajta, bez kamiona, zgrade ili pejzaža.
- Tekst zauzima rezervisan prostor, bez prelaska preko mehanizma i tereta.

## Četiri faze

1. Pozicija: cijeli kran i visoko podignuta paleta; vidi se cilj spuštanja.
2. Spuštanje: uže se produžava, paleta se kontrolisano spušta; blago njihanje.
3. Dosjedanje: pokret usporava, sjenka se približava paleti, teret stiže do cilja.
4. Završetak: paleta miruje, ovjes popušta; sadržaj prirodno prelazi u katalog.

Dužinu scene opravdavaju ove radnje. Eventualno kadriranje prati teret uz isti
prostorni odnos krana i odredišta, bez proizvoljnog pomjeranja cijele ilustracije.
Normalan skrol ostaje uslov; tehnička realizacija kadriranja slijedi usvojenu scenografiju.

## Prompt za storyboard

Create a polished ART DIRECTION STORYBOARD for a website scroll animation, not a finished webpage and not a location photograph. Use the provided isolated realistic 3D crane illustration and pallet illustration as exact subject/style references. Landscape presentation board, 2x2 equal panels on warm off-white #EFE9DD with very thin understated panel dividers. Four panels show FOUR MOMENTS IN ONE COHERENT PHYSICAL SCENE. Fixed camera, identical framing, identical crane scale and position in ALL four panels: full rust-red articulated crane stands firmly on its rotating pedestal at lower left, arm reaches across upper center to the right, a single pallet of ivory construction boards hangs beneath the arm on the right. Reserve quiet negative space above the machinery. No truck, building, landscape, workers, sky, environment, decorative shapes or extra objects. Grounding only through a subtle small contact shadow beneath the pedestal and a tiny horizontal landing mark beneath the intended pallet destination. Stage 1: pallet suspended high at the right, cable taut, arm positioned. Stage 2: SAME crane in SAME place, cable lengthened and pallet halfway down in a strictly vertical trajectory. Stage 3: SAME crane position, pallet almost at the landing mark, controlled careful lowering. Stage 4: SAME crane position, pallet resting at the landing mark, cable and slings relaxed. The crane NEVER floats, translates or flies across the panels. Only its articulated upper arm can subtly adjust and the pallet lowers. Clear, physically coherent rigging, one continuous cable joining pulley and hook. Premium realistic 3D editorial illustration, visibly designed CGI assets rather than real-world photography. Small tasteful charcoal sans-serif labels at each panel top left, exact text: '01 — POZICIJA', '02 — SPUŠTANJE', '03 — DOSJEDANJE', '04 — ZAVRŠETAK'. No other text or logos. The board must communicate a stable stage and an intentional lowering sequence, not random parallax.
