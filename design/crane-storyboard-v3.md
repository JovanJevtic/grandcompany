## Prompt

Create a NEW expanded EIGHT-FRAME cinematic storyboard for a scroll-driven website animation. The reference image establishes the premium realistic 3D miniature illustration style, yellow lattice tower crane design and pallet material ONLY. Change its staging and narrative as instructed below. Wide large high-resolution board, 4 columns x 2 rows, eight equally sized clearly readable panels in chronological left-to-right order. Warm ivory page color #EFE9DD; isolated 3D rendered objects, no photographic scenery. Consistent recognizable yellow tower construction crane with lattice mast, rotating upper jib, counterweight, cabin, hook and one strapped pallet of ivory construction boards. Architectural product-render quality, realistic metal, concrete and wood textures. CRITICAL: panels 01 through 05 contain ONLY the crane and suspended pallet, with its intrinsic support base, and absolutely NO construction site, floor slab, bricks, building, columns, surrounding landscape or other props. The sequence is a deliberate orbit around a crane anchored to its vertical mast, combined with rotation of its upper jib around the mast; no translating or flying of the crane across the composition. Show clearly different camera azimuths and perspectives while preserving the same crane and cargo. Panel 01: wide front three-quarter hero view, entire yellow crane and high suspended pallet isolated on ivory; site absent. Panel 02: orbit toward the side, jib slews slightly, pallet follows beneath trolley, all isolated; site absent. Panel 03: dramatic rear three-quarter angle emphasizing counterweight and cab, pallet still visible hanging off the far jib; site absent. Panel 04: low upward view alongside lattice mast toward overhead jib and suspended pallet, strong depth, no site. Panel 05: return toward a wide elevated three-quarter view, camera begins following the pallet downward; site still completely absent. Panel 06: ONLY NOW the miniature construction site begins to assemble below the still suspended pallet: a compact concrete foundation slab and a few masonry courses emerging into place, partial assembly visibly incomplete. Panel 07: site finishes assembling: low unfinished brick walls and two concrete columns rise, small stacks of timber and blocks at perimeter, clear landing area in center; pallet now being lowered close to this area. Panel 08: beautiful final close view of the completed mini construction-site diorama, pallet rests firmly on the slab, slings slack, lower crane mast visible at the side and hoist cable coming from above. Make camera changes substantial and cinematic, not eight identical frontal pictures. Keep lifting rigging physically coherent and the load level. Use restrained dark sans-serif labels only, exact text per panel: '01 — KRAN I TERET', '02 — ROTACIJA', '03 — DRUGI UGAO', '04 — POGLED ODOZDO', '05 — PRATIMO TERET', '06 — NASTAJE GRADILIŠTE', '07 — SPUŠTANJE', '08 — ISPORUČENO'. No other text, no logo or watermark. Enough margins to avoid cropping labels. The site must appear late, as a payoff, not at the start.
