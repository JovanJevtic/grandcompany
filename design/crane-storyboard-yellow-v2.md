## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a premium 4-panel ART DIRECTION STORYBOARD for a construction-materials website scroll animation. Landscape board, 2x2 equally sized panels, warm ivory background #EFE9DD, fine subtle separators. Realistic high-end 3D miniature architectural illustration with convincing metal, concrete, timber and masonry textures, visibly a crafted rendered scene, NOT a real-world location photograph and NOT a flat vector cartoon. MAIN SUBJECT: a recognizable YELLOW TOWER CONSTRUCTION CRANE, with lattice mast, long horizontal lattice jib, counter-jib and counterweights, vertical hoist cable and hook. Not a red hydraulic loader arm. One strapped wooden pallet of neatly stacked ivory gypsum boards hangs from its cable. SCENOGRAPHY: one coherent mini construction site, an isolated architectural diorama on a compact concrete slab, an unfinished low brick wall and two concrete columns, a short neat stack of blocks and a few timber beams at the perimeter. Leave the central landing area clear. No people, trucks, city, sky or full landscape. Small soft contact shadows ground the miniature on the page. The mini construction site is the intentional destination for the pallet. PANEL 1: wide establishing composition, entire yellow tower crane visible on the left, suspended pallet high on the right; miniature site below is small and understated. PANEL 2: the same crane and scene, pallet descending vertically toward the site, slightly closer framing. PANEL 3: camera gently follows the descending pallet, mini construction site is now prominent in the lower half, crane mast remains visible on the left and cable clearly comes from above; pallet hovers just above its marked clear landing place on the concrete slab. PANEL 4: same landing composition, pallet rests firmly on the slab inside the miniature site, slings relaxed, crane mast and hoist still provide context. Preserve site layout, pallet identity, lighting, yellow crane design and physical scale relationships between panels. The camera follows the load deliberately; the crane's base never moves around the site. Natural lifting geometry, consistent depth and perspective. Restrained graphite sans-serif labels at the top left of each panel, exact text: '01 — KRAN I TERET', '02 — SPUŠTANJE', '03 — MINI GRADILIŠTE', '04 — ISPORUČENO'. No other text, no logos, no watermark. Make the yellow crane and final miniature construction site visually compelling and the descent sequence immediately understandable.
